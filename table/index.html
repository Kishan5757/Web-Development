Zoro:-
Roronoa Zoro is the swordsman of the Straw Hat Pirates, known for his dream of becoming the world's greatest swordsman. He fights using the unique Three-Sword Style (Santoryu), wielding a sword in each hand and one in his mouth. Zoro is fiercely loyal to Luffy and has a strong sense of honor, often training relentlessly to grow stronger.
